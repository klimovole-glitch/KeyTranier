## Morning in the City
The city wakes up early. The first buses leave the depot, street cleaners sweep the sidewalks, and lights come on in the windows of small bakeries. People hurry to work with cups of hot coffee in their hands. In an hour the streets will be full of noise, but right now you can hear the birds singing in the yard.

## Touch Typing
Touch typing lets you write text without looking at the keyboard. Each finger is responsible for its own group of keys, and the hands always return to the home row. At first your speed will be low, but after a few weeks of regular practice the movements become natural and you stop thinking about where each letter is.

## The Autumn Forest
In October the forest looks like a painting. Birch trees stand in yellow, maple leaves turn bright red, and the dark pines seem even more serious. Leaves rustle under your feet, and the air smells of mushrooms and wet earth. If you stop and listen, you can hear a woodpecker somewhere high in the branches.

## How to Learn Something New
To master a new skill, it helps to break it into small steps. Spend at least twenty minutes on it every day instead of several hours once a week. Pay attention to your mistakes and work on them first. Then you will notice real progress within a month.

## The River
A river begins as a small stream somewhere in the mountains. At first it runs fast and loud, jumping over stones and foaming on the rapids. Then it reaches the plain, becomes wider and calmer, and slowly flows past fields and villages. At the end of its journey it meets the sea and gives it all the water collected along the way.
